Guidebook
From Tutorial
--------
WASD to Move
Space to Jump
Shift to Rest
R to sheathe wielded item and go unarmed
Middle Mouse Button to throw away wielded item and go unarmed (this can be used to damage enemies)
Left Click while unarmed to interact with objects or pick up items

Basics
--------
Don't die! death is permanent, unless you pay for insurance in town.
Time runs when transitioning map. Time wasted means more cost to pay for food.
Watch for wandering trainers in town if you are favoring certain specialization.
Access more information from the town library.
